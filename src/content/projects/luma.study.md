A place to keep lecture slides, notes, and past questions so they're actually findable when exam season arrives. The AI assistant answers questions about the material you've uploaded — no hallucinated answers about things that aren't in your notes.

## Why

The default for most students is a folder on Google Drive named "SCHOOL STUFF" with 400 files in it. This is the opposite of that.

*Note*: There's no available frontend for this project at the moment.
